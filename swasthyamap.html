🧭 How SwasthyaMap Works

A simple healthcare discovery workflow.

1️⃣ Detect

Allow browser location access to find nearby healthcare services.

2️⃣ Discover

Choose hospitals, pharmacies, diagnostics, scans, blood banks or pet care.

3️⃣ Connect

Open the existing SwasthyaMitra page for the service you need.
