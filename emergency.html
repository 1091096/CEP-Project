🆘 Basic emergency first aid

Simple guidance while professional medical help is being arranged.

01

Severe bleeding

Apply firm, continuous pressure with clean cloth or gauze. Keep pressure on the wound while arranging emergency care.

Do not remove deeply embedded objects.
02

Unconscious person

Check responsiveness and breathing. Call emergency services and follow dispatcher instructions.

Do not give food, water or medicines to an unconscious person.
03

Chest pain

Stop exertion, keep the person comfortable and seek emergency assessment for severe or new chest pain.

Call emergency services for severe, persistent or concerning symptoms.
04

Possible stroke

Sudden facial drooping, arm weakness or speech difficulty requires urgent emergency evaluation.

Note the time symptoms started if known.
